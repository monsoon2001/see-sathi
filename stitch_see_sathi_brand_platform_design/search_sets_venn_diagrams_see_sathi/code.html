Q11: Survey of 100 students on tea & coffee consumption with cardinality constraints

“In a survey of 100 secondary school students, 65 like tea, 45 like coffee, and 15 like neither of the drinks. (i) Represent the above information in a Venn diagram. (ii) Find the number of students who like both drinks. (iii) Find the ratio of students who like tea only to coffee only.”

1 Let n(U) = 100, n(T) = 65, n(C) = 45, n(T∪C)' = 15
2 n(T∪C) = n(U) - n(T∪C)' = 100 - 15 = 85
✓ n(T∩C) = 65 + 45 - 85 = 25 students
