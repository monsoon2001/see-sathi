## Brand & Style

This design system positions the platform as an elite, focused academic companion for high-school students preparing for the Class 10 Secondary Education Examination (SEE). Drawing inspiration from modern productivity platforms like Linear, Notion, and Superhuman, the aesthetic rejects the childish, gamified clutter typical of standard EdTech apps in favor of calm clarity, mechanical precision, and warm academic warmth.

The brand persona balances two core traits:
1. **The Modern High-Performance Workspace:** Monochromatic structural foundations, tight information density, keyboard-first velocity, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy that honors the student’s intellect.
2. **The Supportive Companion ("Sathi"):** Welcoming warm paper tones, micro-affirmations, tactile feedback, and uplifting energy that relieves exam anxiety.

Visual execution relies on a high-contrast foundation punctuated by a luminous Electric Indigo core, calibrated subject-level accents, soft colored glow elevations, and crisp pill contours.

## Colors

The palette establishes a high-contrast, tactile reading and learning environment reminiscent of high-grade editorial paper paired with glowing modern software accents.

### Core Canvas & Structure
- **Ink Primary (`#0E1020`):** Ultra-deep navy-black used for primary text, high-emphasis headings, active icons, and high-contrast dark badges.
- **Ink Muted (`#5B5E6E`):** Mid-tone neutral used for secondary text, metadata, deactivated states, and descriptive labels.
- **Line Subtle (`rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.08)`): Deliberate boundary definition without heavy visual division.
- **Paper White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level component surface, elevated floating cards, and input fields.
- **Paper Warm (`#F7F6F2`):** Primary canvas background, reducing eye strain during multi-hour study sessions.

### Functional & Interaction Accents
- **Brand Primary (Electric Indigo - `#4B4FF2`):** Focal interactive elements, progress completions, key CTA buttons, active tab indicators, and current session anchors.
- **Brand Secondary (Vivid Coral - `#FF5A3C`):** Streaks, countdown timers, critical alerts, attention tags, and real-time urgency.
- **Brand Success (Fresh Green - `#2FB673`):** Verified correct solutions, completed modules, mastery indicators, and upward progress deltas.

### Dedicated Subject Palette
Subject identity must remain consistent across problem banks, syllabus trackers, and flashcards:
- **Mathematics:** `#4B4FF2` (Indigo)
- **Science:** `#2FB673` (Green)
- **English:** `#FF5A3C` (Coral)
- **Nepali:** `#EAA412` (Amber)
- **Social Studies:** `#E0457B` (Rose)
- **Optional Math:** `#0EA5E9` (Sky)
- **Computer Science:** `#6366F1` (Violet)

Every subject color is applied with a 12% tint background fill coupled with the solid token for text and icon strokes.

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural, confident geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the analytical, highly legible utility of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Driven by intentional negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to generate the tight, confident cadence seen in high-end software tools. Headlines never flow loosely; they lock together visually as solid graphic anchors.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts (Inter):** Configured for long-form study, revision notes, and technical solution steps. Clear separation between numbers prevents ambiguous formula reads.
- **Editorial Micro-Copy (`label-caps`):** Used strictly for metadata tags, subject categories, countdown timer labels, and question difficulty indicators. Always rendered in uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.06em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a strict 12-column grid on desktop layouts and an adaptable single/dual-column canvas on mobile devices, maintaining unified horizontal rhythm.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (`max-w-7xl`, 1280px) centered on `Paper Warm` canvas. Sidebar anchors navigation at a fixed 260px, while the study canvas operates across 8 to 9 columns, with the contextual helper/formula tray utilizing the remaining columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins. Toolbars and metadata condense into horizontal segmented action strips.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px screen edge margins (`margin-sm`). Secondary utilities compress into bottom action drawers.

### Spacing Principles
Component nesting relies on strict multi-tier padding: outer card padding is fixed to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile. Interior item stacks maintain a uniform `space-sm` (8px) or `space-xs` (4px) cadence to preserve compact visual density.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent paper layers, razor-thin low-contrast outlines, and signature tinted ambient glows that highlight active progress.

### 1. The Canvas Stack
- **Floor (`#F7F6F2`):** Non-elevated warm matte canvas.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** High-grade floating elements, quiz panels, and module summaries. Uses border-stroke `rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.06)` and shadow `0 2px 4px rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.03), 0 12px 32px rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Glass Hover / Active Modal):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `16px`, bordered by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on top edge and `rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.08)` on bottom/sides.

### 2. Colored Glow Shadows
Interactive elements avoid pure black drop shadows:
- **Indigo Glow (Primary Focus):** `0 4px 14px rgba(75, 79, 242, 0.28), 0 1px 2px rgba(75, 79, 242, 0.16)`
- **Coral Glow (Streak / Urgency):** `0 4px 14px rgba(255, 90, 60, 0.28), 0 1px 2px rgba(255, 90, 60, 0.16)`
- **Success Glow (Solved / Mastered):** `0 4px 14px rgba(47, 182, 115, 0.25)`

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined pill-dominant geometry (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Controls & Buttons:** Strictly pill-shaped (`9999px` / `rounded-full`). All interactive click triggers, chips, badges, and search bars inherit complete semicircular edge terminators.
- **Card Containers & Modules:** Standardized to `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for desktop canvases, softening to `1rem` (16px) on mobile cards to optimize content real-estate.
- **Inner Micro-Elements (Input Fields & Nested Rows):** Styled with `0.75rem` (12px) to maintain nested proportional harmony with outer `24px` card corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background `Electric Indigo` (`#4B4FF2`), text `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600 weight. Features the signature Indigo ambient glow shadow. On hover, translates `-1px` with increased glow `rgba(75, 79, 242, 0.38)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill:** Background `rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.04)`, text `#0E1020`, border `1px solid rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.08)`. Hover changes background to `rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.08)`.
- **Destructive / Urgency Pill:** Background `Vivid Coral` (`#FF5A3C`), white text, Coral ambient glow shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Subject Tag:** Pill shape with height of 28px, uppercase `label-caps`, 12px horizontal padding. Composed of a 12% tint background of the respective subject color, solid subject color text, and a matching 30% alpha border.
- **Duotone Icon Badges:** Symmetrical 40px or 48px square container with continuous rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`). Contains a 2-tone icon combining 20% opacity fills with solid color active vector paths.

### Cards & Question Surfaces
- **Interactive Question Card:** Base of `#FFFFFF`, 24px border radius, enclosed in a 1px border of `rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.06)`. Contains a header bar separating the subject label and question weightage in marks.
- **Floating Glass Companion Card:** Backed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Elevated with a multi-layered soft drop shadow.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Input Fields:** 48px height, 12px corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0E1020`, placeholder `#5B5E6E`. Resting border `1.5px solid rgba(14, 16, 32, 0.1)`. Focus state transitions border to `#4B4FF2` along with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(75, 79, 242, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Multiple Choice Radio / Checkbox Tiles:** Pill-bordered or rounded-xl selection tiles. Resting state has `#FFFFFF` background with subtle outline. Selected state swaps to `rgba(75, 79, 242, 0.06)` fill, solid `#4B4FF2` border, and animated radio icon fill.
- **Solution Verification States:** Instant transition to `#2FB673` border and soft green tint upon correct validation; transition to `#FF5A3C` on incorrect selection.

### Study-Specific Components
- **Streak & Companion Tracker:** Persistent pill widget exhibiting current day streak count with an inline flame vector in Vivid Coral, resting on a frosted white pill surface.
- **Formula & Reference Drawer:** Side-docked utilitarian strip with monospaced reference tokens and quick-copy action handles.
