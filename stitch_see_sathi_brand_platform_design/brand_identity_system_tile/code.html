Q18: State Ohm's Law. Derive the mathematical formula connecting potential difference (V), current (I), and resistance (R).

Asked in SEE 2079 (Bagmati Province), SEE 2076 (Gandaki). Essential for Group C Physics section.

Model Answer Framework Mark Distribution: 1 + 3 = 4

Statement: “Under constant physical conditions (such as temperature), the electric current passing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across its terminals.”

I ∝ V  ⇒  V = I · R  ⇒  R = V / I
